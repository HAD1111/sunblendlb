About Sunblend

Rooted in Lebanon, made for the modern pantry.

Sunblend specializes in premium healthy food products — dried fruits, dried vegetables, fruit bars, and traditional Lebanese staples. Every product is chosen for its quality, prepared simply, and blended with nothing artificial, so what reaches you is close to what came from the tree.
